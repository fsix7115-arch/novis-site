About

What is Novis?

Novis is a free, friendly place to meet new people face to face. One tap starts an anonymous video conversation with someone you have never met — no profile, no pressure, just a real moment with a real person.

Made for real moments

Sometimes the best conversation is with someone completely new. Novis exists for those small, genuine moments — a laugh, a story, a few kind words from across the world that make the day feel a little less lonely.

Kindness first, always

Everyone on Novis deserves to be treated with respect. The app is strictly 18+, and report and block tools are always one tap away — if a conversation ever stops feeling good, you can leave or skip on, instantly and without explanation.

Free, and open to everyone

Novis costs nothing, and you do not need an account to try it. There is no sign-up, no profile to build and nothing to pay — open the app, tap once, and say hello to someone new.
